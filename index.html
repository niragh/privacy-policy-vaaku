1. Overview & Data Controller

This Privacy Policy explains how Vaakku ("we", "our", or "the App") collects, uses, and shares your information when you install and use the Vaakku Android application (package name: com.vaakku.app).

Vaakku is a bilingual word puzzle game for Android, featuring Malayalam and English puzzles, daily 5-word challenges, endless progression levels, and weekly leaderboards.

For questions or requests concerning your data, you can contact the developer team at support@vaakku.app (or via our GitHub repository issue tracker).

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum data required to deliver core puzzle gameplay, persist your achievements, and serve relevant advertisements.

A. Local Gameplay Data (On-Device)

When you play Vaakku, the app records gameplay progress directly on your phone using Android Jetpack DataStore and Room database:

  • Current level number and completed level history
  • Daily puzzle progress and completion dates
  • Current and best consecutive daily streak count
  • In-game coin balance and hint usage
  • Player preferences (chosen language: English or Malayalam; haptics vibration on/off)

This local data is stored in the application's private sandbox storage on your device.

B. Optional Account Data (Google Sign-In & Firebase Auth)

You may choose to sign in using your Google Account through Google Credential Manager and Firebase Authentication. If you do:

  • Account Identifiers: Your unique Google User ID, email address, and display name.
  • Profile Picture: Your public Google profile avatar URL (used for your leaderboard avatar).

Note: If you do not sign in, you can play entirely as a Guest. No account identifiers or personal profiles are collected.

C. Leaderboard & Cloud Synchronization Data (Firebase Firestore)

When signed in, your weekly solved puzzle points and leaderboard display name are synchronized with Google Cloud Firestore so you can view your standing on the weekly leaderboard (which resets every Monday).

D. Advertising & Diagnostic Identifiers (Google AdMob)

The free tier of Vaakku displays advertisements powered by Google AdMob. The Google Mobile Ads SDK and User Messaging Platform (UMP) may automatically collect:

  • Google Advertising ID (GAID / AAID)
  • Coarse location derived from IP address (used for geographic ad delivery, e.g. country or region)
  • Device information (manufacturer, device model, operating system version, screen size)
  • Ad performance metrics (views, clicks, and rewarded ad completion events)
  • Consent status records under the IAB TCF framework (EEA/UK users)

E. Analytics & Crash Information (Google Analytics for Firebase)

To help us detect bugs and understand feature adoption, Firebase Analytics collects pseudonymized events such as app launch frequency, level completion rates, and crash reports. This data is aggregated and does not identify individual players personally.

F. Device Permissions

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Used to download daily puzzles, fetch leaderboard scores, authenticate with Google, and display AdMob ads.
  • android.permission.VIBRATE: Used exclusively to provide subtle tactile haptic feedback when tapping letter tiles or solving words. This can be toggled off at any time in the in-app Settings.

3. How We Use Your Information

We process the collected data for the following lawful and legitimate purposes:

  • Deliver Gameplay: To load deterministic daily puzzles, validate word tiles, manage streaks, and award virtual coins.
  • Cloud Sync: To restore your earned levels and streak when switching devices (for signed-in Google users).
  • Leaderboard Competition: To calculate weekly points and display rankings among players.
  • Monetization & Free Availability: To show advertisements via Google AdMob that keep the game free to play for everyone.
  • User Consent Enforcement: To verify whether users in the European Economic Area (EEA) and the UK have agreed to personalized or non-personalized ads in compliance with GDPR.
  • App Quality & Diagnostics: To detect crashes, improve puzzle difficulty balance, and maintain app stability.

5. Advertising & Consent Choices

Ad placements in Vaakku are intentionally engineered to comply with the Google AdMob Rewarded Ad Policy and Google Play Developer Policies:

Format Placement Rule & Frequency
Banner Ad Home Screen Bottom Only visible on the Home screen; never on the puzzle board near touchable tiles.
Interstitial Ad Level Transitions Shown at most once every 3 solved levels and at least 90 seconds apart. Never shown during puzzle solving or at app launch.
Rewarded Ad In-Game Hint Sheet Strictly user-opted. You only see a rewarded ad if you explicitly tap "Watch a short ad for a free hint".
How to Manage or Revoke Your Ad Consent

If you are in the European Economic Area (EEA) or the United Kingdom, Google UMP presents a consent dialog on your first launch. You can review or change your consent choices at any time inside the app:

Open Vaakku → Tap Settings (⚙️ icon) → Tap "Privacy options" under Privacy.

Personalized Ads Opt-Out (Android OS Level)

You can also reset or opt-out of interest-based advertising across all Android apps on your device:

  1. Go to your phone's Settings.
  2. Navigate to Google → All services → Ads (or Privacy → Ads).
  3. Tap "Delete advertising ID" or toggle "Opt out of Ads Personalization".

6. Storage, Security & Retention

Local Data: Stored locally in your phone's secure sandboxed internal storage. Clearing the app's cache/data or uninstalling the app permanently deletes local game progress.

Cloud Data: For users signed in with Google, Firebase data is hosted in Google Cloud data centers protected by Google's industry-standard physical, electronic, and administrative safeguards. All communications between the app and Firebase / AdMob occur over encrypted HTTPS / TLS connections.

Retention Period: Local progress remains as long as the app is installed. Leaderboard points are cleared automatically every Monday at the weekly reset.

7. Your Rights & Data Deletion

Depending on your jurisdiction (such as under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), or India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP)), you possess specific privacy rights:

  • Right to Access: You can view your current streak, level, coins, and leaderboard score directly within the app.
  • Right to Rectification: You can update your display name or avatar via your Google Account.
  • Right to Erasure (Data Deletion): You have the right to request deletion of all cloud account and leaderboard records associated with your account.
How to Request Account & Data Deletion

To delete your data from Vaakku:
1. Local Data: Clear the app's storage in Android Settings → Apps → Vaakku → Storage → Clear Data, or uninstall the app.
2. Cloud Account & Leaderboard Data: Send an email to support@vaakku.app with the subject line "Vaakku Data Deletion Request" from the Google email address used to sign in. We will permanently delete your Firestore records and Firebase authentication profile within 14 business days.

8. Children's Privacy

Vaakku is designed as a family-friendly educational puzzle game, but our target audience is players 13 years of age or older (or 16 in certain European jurisdictions).

We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal identifiable information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child under 13 has provided personal information to us without parental consent, please contact us at support@vaakku.app. We will promptly delete that information from our servers.

11. മലയാളം ചുരുക്കം (Summary in Malayalam)

വാക്ക് (Vaakku) ആപ്പ് ഉപയോഗിക്കുന്നവർക്കായി സ്വകാര്യതാ വിവരങ്ങൾ ചുരുക്കത്തിൽ:

1. ഞങ്ങൾ ശേഖരിക്കുന്ന വിവരങ്ങൾ

  • കളിയുടെ വിവരങ്ങൾ: നിങ്ങൾ കണ്ടെത്തിയ വാക്കുകൾ, ദിവസേനയുള്ള സ്ട്രീക്ക് (Streak), കൈവശമുള്ള നാണയങ്ങൾ (Coins), ലെവലുകൾ എന്നിവ നിങ്ങളുടെ ഫോണിൽ സുരക്ഷിതമായി സൂക്ഷിക്കുന്നു.
  • ഗൂഗിൾ സൈൻ ഇൻ (ഐച്ഛികം): ഗൂഗിൾ അക്കൗണ്ട് ഉപയോഗിച്ച് സൈൻ ഇൻ ചെയ്താൽ മാത്രമേ പേരും ഇമെയിൽ വിലാസവും ക്ലൗഡിൽ സൂക്ഷിക്കപ്പെടുന്നുള്ളൂ. ഇത് ഫോൺ മാറുമ്പോഴും കളിയുടെ വിവരങ്ങൾ നഷ്ടപ്പെടാതിരിക്കാനും പ്രതിവാര ലീഡർബോർഡിൽ പേര് കാണിക്കാനും മാത്രമാണ്. ഗസ്റ്റ് ആയും ആപ്പ് ഉപയോഗിക്കാം.
  • പരസ്യങ്ങൾ: ആപ്പ് സൗജന്യമായി നൽകുന്നതിനായി ഗൂഗിൾ ആഡ്‌മോബ് (AdMob) പരസ്യങ്ങൾ ഉപയോഗിക്കുന്നു. ഇതിനായി പരസ്യ ഐഡി (Advertising ID) ഗൂഗിൾ ഉപയോഗിച്ചേക്കാം.

2. ഞങ്ങൾ ചെയ്യാത്ത കാര്യങ്ങൾ

  • നിങ്ങളുടെ കോൺടാക്റ്റുകൾ, ക്യാമറ, മൈക്രോഫോൺ, ഫോട്ടോകൾ, വ്യക്തിഗത ലൊക്കേഷൻ എന്നിവ ഈ ആപ്പ് ആവശ്യപ്പെടുകയോ ഉപയോഗിക്കുകയോ ചെയ്യുന്നില്ല.

3. പരസ്യ സമ്മതം മാറ്റാൻ

ആപ്പിലെ Settings (ക്രമീകരണങ്ങൾ) → Privacy options (സ്വകാര്യതാ ഓപ്ഷനുകൾ) വഴി നിങ്ങൾക്ക് പരസ്യ സമ്മതം എപ്പോൾ വേണമെങ്കിലും മാറ്റാവുന്നതാണ്.

4. വിവരങ്ങൾ ഒഴിവാക്കാൻ (Data Deletion)

ഫോണിലെ ആപ്പ് ഡാറ്റ മായ്ക്കുകയോ ആപ്പ് അൺഇൻസ്റ്റാൾ ചെയ്യുകയോ ചെയ്താൽ വിവരങ്ങൾ ഒഴിവാകും. ഗൂഗിൾ അക്കൗണ്ടുമായി ബന്ധപ്പെട്ട ലീഡർബോർഡ് വിവരങ്ങൾ നീക്കം ചെയ്യാൻ support@vaakku.app എന്ന വിലാസത്തിലേക്ക് ഇമെയിൽ അയക്കാവുന്നതാണ്.
